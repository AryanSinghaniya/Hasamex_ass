Expert Interview Guide — Robotic Surgery Market Research
=========================================================

This guide is used across all expert interviews. Questions are numbered 1–6.
Interviewers may probe deeper or adjust phrasing, but must cover all 6 areas.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. ADOPTION BARRIERS
   What are the primary barriers to the adoption of robotic surgery systems
   in your market? Please consider capital cost, infrastructure, clinical
   evidence, and any regulatory or cultural factors that are specific to
   your country.

2. REIMBURSEMENT LANDSCAPE
   How does the current reimbursement environment affect the uptake of
   robotic surgical procedures? Are there specific procedure types or
   specialties where reimbursement coverage is stronger or weaker, and
   how do you expect this to evolve over the next two to three years?

3. COMPETITIVE DYNAMICS
   How do you assess the competitive landscape among the major robotic
   surgery platforms in your market — including both established players
   and emerging entrants? Which factors (clinical outcomes, service,
   pricing, ecosystem lock-in) most influence platform selection?

4. SURGEON TRAINING & ADOPTION PATHWAY
   What does the surgeon training pathway look like for robotic systems,
   and how does it influence which platform a hospital ultimately chooses?
   Are there training bottlenecks or credentialing challenges that slow
   adoption?

5. HOSPITAL PROCUREMENT DECISIONS
   How do hospitals in your market structure procurement decisions for
   robotic systems? Who are the key stakeholders (clinical, finance,
   management), what is the typical decision timeline, and what role do
   lease/subscription models play versus outright capital purchase?

6. FUTURE OUTLOOK
   What is your outlook for robotic surgery in your market over the next
   three to five years? Which specialties do you expect to grow fastest,
   and what technological or policy developments could meaningfully
   accelerate or hinder that growth?
